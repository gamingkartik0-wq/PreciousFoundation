About Us

A caring place to call home.

PRECIOUS FOUNDATION is a non-profit organization dedicated to providing care, dignity, love and support to senior citizens and people in need.

Our Old Age Home in Kokrajhar, BTC, Assam, aims to provide a safe, peaceful and comfortable environment where elderly people can live with respect and dignity.

We believe every elderly person deserves affection, companionship, proper care, nutritious food and a secure place to call home.

Our dedicated team works to support daily needs while creating a caring, family-like atmosphere.
